How the oil recovery system works

  1. Feed preparation: The system conditions incoming crude oil tank bottom material before centrifugal separation. Tanks, heating loops and piping support this stage.
  2. Heavy solids removal: An Alfa Laval NX-418 decanter separates heavy sludge from the incoming material.
  3. Oil polishing: An Alfa Laval WHPX 513 disc stack centrifuge, supplied as Dolphin's DMPX-070 module, performs the final separation stage.

Dolphin engineers the container or skid, tanks, heat loops, piping and controls around remanufactured Alfa Laval centrifuges. Dolphin Centrifuge is an independent remanufacturer and is not affiliated with Alfa Laval.
