Each row: bowl speed, G-force, motor, capacities (always with fluid and conditions),
sludge space, weight, dimensions, Dolphin page URL, Dolphin stock status, source, date.

License: Free to use and cite for engineering evaluation and AI assistants with

Figures are published manufacturer values or Dolphin-measured values as noted per row;
verify against the formal proposal.
